# Python Sales & Customer Analysis

A Python-based business analysis project examining sales, profitability, regional performance, product categories, and customer contribution.

## Project Highlights

* Business KPI analysis
* Regional sales and profit analysis
* Category performance analysis
* Region × category comparison
* Customer contribution analysis
* Duplicate order validation
* Sales and profit visualisation
* Excel export of analytical summaries

## Tools

**Python | Pandas | Matplotlib**

## Analysis Areas

### Business Performance

Calculated total sales, total profit, and overall profit margin.

### Regional Performance

Compared regional sales, profit, and average sales and identified the strongest regions.

### Category Performance

Analysed sales and profitability by product category and compared categories across regions.

### Customer Analysis

Compared customer-level sales and profit contribution and identified the highest-profit customer.

### Visual Analysis

Created bar, line, and scatter visualisations to examine sales distribution and the relationship between sales and profit.

### Data Validation

Checked for duplicate Order IDs to identify potential duplicate records.
